# Code of conduct

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and
any other space managed by the Grintion-OS project.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith. Ask what someone meant before assuming the worst reading.
- Critique code, claims and evidence, not people.
- Keep reports reproducible and honest. Mark untested work as untested; do not
  describe an assumption as a verified result.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change that widens the image, weakens a
  check, or adds a claim the project cannot prove.
- Respect the time of unpaid maintainers. A unanswered question is not an
  insult.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language about who someone is.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Pressuring maintainers for unpaid work, immediate fixes, or roadmap
  commitments.
- Deliberately wasting review time: spam, duplicate reports, bad-faith
  argument, or reopening a settled decision without new evidence.
- Using project spaces to advertise a product or a paid service.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainer through GitHub
([@Freddyz-coder](https://github.com/Freddyz-coder)). Do not open a public issue
about another person's behaviour.

Reports are handled on a best-effort basis. Depending on severity, the response
ranges from a private warning to editing or removing content to a temporary or
permanent ban from project spaces. The maintainer will explain the decision to
the person affected when it is safe to do so.

## Scope

This code covers project spaces. It does not govern private conversations or
other projects, and it is not a substitute for the law.
